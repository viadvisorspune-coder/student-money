# Chip

A pill that holds a short fact: a date, a duration, a trend or a time slot.

- **Consumer provides:** `children` (two or three words at most) and, optionally, `icon` and `trend`.
- `soft` goes on `surface`, `light` on `surface-soft` and colour cards, and `dark` is the time pill at the top of a detail sheet.
- A trend chip always pairs a sign with an icon: “+15%” with `trend="up"`, “−₹450” with `trend="down"`.
