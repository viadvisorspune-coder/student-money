# InsightCard

One observed pattern, written as a comparison with the student's own past behaviour or with a context.

- **Consumer provides:** `kind` (“You vs yourself” or “This changes when…”), `children` (the sentence) and an optional `detail`.
- Only two kinds of insight are allowed: comparing the student with their own history, and showing when behaviour changes. Never compare them with other people, judge them, or tell them what to do — those backfire.
- Every sentence names real figures from their own data.
