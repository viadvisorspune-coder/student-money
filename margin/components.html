Components

Every small part a page is assembled from. Each is built only from the foundations: one typeface, seven colours, the route. Classes live in css/components.css. Sample content throughout is illustrative, not study data.

04.5

Components

Underlines & marks

Ways to point at one word inside running text. Use one per paragraph at most, and only on the word the paragraph turns on.

MarkerYellow highlighter behind the word. The insight word. .m-mark

P02 knew about Friday, but it was not part of the decision.

Route underlineSolid red, route-weight. An event or intervention. .m-u-route

Then their friends suggested ordering.

Hand strokeA drawn line under a word the researcher wants to flag. .m-u-hand

“I'll adjust later,” said four of the six.

Hand circleA loose ring around one word or figure. .m-circle

The dinner was ₹1,500, already in their plans.

WaveUncertain or disputed phrasing. .m-u-wave

Participants said the app felt judgemental, though none used that word first.

DashedA hypothesis in the text. .m-u-dash

We expected that seeing the future would change the present.

StrikeA position the project abandoned. .m-strike

V0 told students to spend less. V3 shows what is coming up.
