Margin

Design system  /  Kharcha research report  /  v0.1

Editorial, archival and a little playful. A system for a user-research report about how students spend, built on one idea: the research itself becomes the graphic language. A day is a line, a spend is a node, a plan is a ghost, a finding is a place the line arrives at.

2026

Six chapters

  1. 01FoundationsTypography, colour, grid, spacing, shape and the route, iconography, imagery, motion.
  2. 02Information grammarEvidence, claims, participants, quotes, observations, insights, decisions, outcomes, uncertainty.
  3. 03Data visualisation18 charts for user-testing data: quantitative, qualitative, timelines, matrices, maps, networks, flows, comparisons, distributions.
  4. 04ComponentsPills, tags, labels, notes, stickers, underlines, headers, footers, folios, callouts, captions, footnotes, legends, tables, navigation, image formats.
  5. 05Page patternsFourteen spreads combining images and text, from chapter opener to conclusion.
  6. 06RulesWhen to use what, what not to do, accessibility, data integrity, citation, composition.

To decide

  • TypefacesMontserrat and Merriweather are the closest free matches to the reference. If the reference's own faces are known and licensed, swap them in tokens.json.
  • Mustard contrastThe reference's mustard is 1.89 : 1 on paper. Kept exact and limited to large headlines; a darker text mustard is available if the report must meet WCAG for all text.
  • Page sizeA4 portrait spreads assumed. The reference book is smaller; the grid scales from tokens.
  • Real dataEvery figure here is sample data, and must be replaced from the study archive.
